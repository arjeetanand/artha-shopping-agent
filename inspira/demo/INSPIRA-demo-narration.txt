Welcome to INSPIRA, an approval-gated AI purchase-agent demo. It helps turn inspiration into a constraint-checked shortlist before anything is ordered.

On the home screen, I choose a demo inspiration image and ask for a relaxed Goa travel outfit, under six thousand rupees, delivered to Bangalore within five days. When configured, the frontend sends this structured request to a Make.com gateway. This recording runs in safe demo mode, so every simulated field remains labelled.

The next screen turns the brief into structured intent: relaxed silhouette, neutral palette, outfit components, budget, and delivery window. Visual interpretations are marked AI inferred, not verified facts.

Now the app searches its demo catalog. It checks total cost, stock, and delivery timing before ranking only the options that pass the hard constraints.

Here are three eligible bundles. Each card shows the total with shipping, delivery days, stock, and match score. Provenance labels distinguish stock and ratings from simulated price, shipping, and delivery estimates.

I select the Sandstone Set. The approval screen presents the item list, final total, destination, and estimated arrival. It does not create an order yet.

Only when I choose Confirm simulated purchase does the system create a mock hackathon order. The confirmation shows a mock order ID and mock payment status. Finally, tracking displays the timeline. Advancing the demo status produces the visible notification event.

INSPIRA completes the journey from inspiration to valid recommendations, explicit approval, a simulated order, and tracking.
