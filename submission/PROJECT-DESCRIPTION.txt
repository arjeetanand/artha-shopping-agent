Artha is an AI shopping research assistant that turns a natural-language brief, reference image, or product link into a source-linked purchase plan. Instead of opening dozens of tabs, shoppers describe their budget, preferences, location, and delivery needs. Artha clarifies missing details, searches retailer sources, compares products, and ranks options against those constraints. Each recommendation keeps supporting links and highlights unknown prices, stock, variants, or delivery information rather than inventing certainty. A conversational interface supports typed and voice interactions, while signed-in users can save private purchase plans. Built with React, a Python backend, SQLite, and configurable AI, search, and speech providers, the prototype explores a more transparent way to shop. Real purchases happen on retailer websites; the local order flow is explicitly labeled as a sandbox. Its evidence gate separates checked recommendations from uncertain options, helping shoppers compare trade-offs and understand each result’s limits before choosing a retailer and proceeding further.
